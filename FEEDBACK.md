# Client feedback

## Second conversation with Adam

**Date:** Wednesday 9/30/2026, the day after the showcase. We talked about the
live site. I did not write it down at the time, so on 10/12 I sent him the list
below and he confirmed it was what he asked for.

## What Adam said about the first version

1. It should not be only for wedding planners. His boards also sell for Father's
   Day, birthdays, reunions and storefront windows.
2. People should be able to order online and upload their photo themselves, not
   only call him. Ordering happens on his main site, printyourday.com, so he
   wants this site to send people there.
3. Keep "you see a proof before we print" up front, because that is what makes
   people trust a print this big.
4. Show the 48 hour rush and the delivery prices.
5. Keep the bigger size, the Jumbo, as a quote.

Points 3, 4 and 5 were already on the site, so they stay as they are. Points 1
and 2 are new.

## The Later list, in Adam's order

1. A quote request form
2. A version of this site for restaurants and storefronts
3. Planner price list
4. Testimonials

PRD.md's Later section is now in this order.

## One decision: the quote request form

Options the agent laid out: a form service like Formspree, a mailto link that
opens an email with the fields filled in, or sending people to the order and
quote pages on printyourday.com. I picked printyourday.com, because Adam already
takes orders and Jumbo quotes there and this site has no server to save a form.

## What changed because of it

The home page now has an "Upload your photo at printyourday.com" link under the
call button, an "order online" link under the prices, and a "Not just weddings"
section for his other events. The phone number is still the main button on every
page.
