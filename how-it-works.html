How it works

  1. Send the photo. A clear shot from a recent phone prints well at this size. If it will not hold up at 40 by 60, Adam tells you before printing, not after.
  2. Approve the proof. The photo is colour corrected and a digital proof is emailed to you. Nothing prints until you say go. If the board does not match the proof, it is reprinted free.
  3. Pick up or get it delivered. Free pickup at the studio in Mount Vernon, NY. Delivery is $75 across Westchester, the Bronx and Fairfield County, and $100 in New York City.

Standard turnaround is seven business days. Need it in two? Rush is $50 a board.
